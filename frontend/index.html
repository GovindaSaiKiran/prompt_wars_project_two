Interactive Experience

Voting Day Simulator

Experience the step-by-step process of casting your vote at a polling station.

Step 1: Polling Booth Entry

Upon arrival at your designated polling station, join the queue. Security personnel will guide you to the entry point of your specific booth.

Step 2: ID & List Verification

The First Polling Officer will check your name on the electoral roll and verify your ID proof (EPIC or other allowed IDs).

Step 3: Indelible Ink & Register

The Second Polling Officer will mark your left forefinger with indelible ink, record your signature/thumb impression, and issue a voter slip.

Step 4: Casting your Vote via EVM

Go to the voting compartment. Press the blue button against your candidate on the EVM. A 'beep' will sound, and you'll see the VVPAT slip verification.
